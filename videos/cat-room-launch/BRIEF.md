## Intent

Launch video for Cat Room (https://rooms.krasnoperov.me): a hand-painted-looking
isometric room rendered live in the browser, with a cat that lives there. The
user asked for a product-launch video "as cool as you can make it". Tone: warm,
lo-fi, playful, a little magical; the room is the star, not UI.

Chosen concept (autonomous pitch): one day in the room, compressed. Morning
light, the cat napping in the sun patch; things dropped in from the dresser
change its behaviour (the box it cannot resist, the yarn, the laser, sparrows at
the feeder); dusk, the lantern and the radio with its notes; end card with the
URL. Left behind: a screen-recorded site tour with a cursor.

## Assets

- capture/assets/clips/*.mp4 — frame-exact live renders of the room made with the
  project's own fixed-step film mode; these are the featured footage.
- ../../audio/golden-hour.mp3 — the room's own generated lo-fi track; the music bed.

## Customizations

- No voice-over; short on-screen captions only.
- Music bed from the room's own soundtrack, not a library track.

## Notes

- Built with Claude (Opus 5.5), Blender models, and makefx for textures, music
  and reference art — may appear as a single credit line near the end.
- Destination is X, but 16:9 matches the reference posts in the thread the user
  is responding to (1920x1080 videos).
